Domain Research

Exploring the academic foundation, technological landscape, and innovative solutions of intelligent retail analytics.

The Problem

Traditional mall advertising is static and lacks personalization. Businesses have no real-time insights into who is looking at their ads, their emotional response, or their engagement levels, leading to wasted marketing spend and missed opportunities.

The Solution

MallVision utilizes advanced Computer Vision and Deep Learning to detect customer demographics, emotions, and engagement in real-time. This data allows the system to serve hyper-personalized advertisements that resonate with the audience.

Literature Survey

Recent advancements in Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence have significantly improved the ability to analyze human behavior through visual data. Prior studies have explored facial detection, demographic estimation (age and gender), and emotion recognition using deep learning models such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) [1], [2], [3].

Research in customer analytics has also evolved with the integration of vision-based systems to understand consumer behavior in retail environments. These systems enable insights into customer preferences, movement patterns, and engagement levels [4].

While these approaches demonstrate the potential of intelligent retail systems, many existing solutions operate in isolated domains, lacking a unified framework that combines real-time perception, behavioral analysis, and adaptive content delivery [6].

Research Gap

Despite notable progress, several limitations remain in existing systems:

  • Lack of real-time personalization in advertising delivery [5]
  • Limited integration of emotion and engagement analysis [3], [6]
  • Absence of returning customer identification mechanisms [1]

These gaps highlight the need for an integrated system capable of performing real-time, multi-dimensional analysis to enhance customer interaction.

Research Objectives

Age and Gender Estimation

Implement a real-time age and gender classification module using deep learning techniques to identify demographic characteristics of mall audiences.

Demographic-Aware Adaptation

Enable advertisement personalization by utilizing predicted age and gender information to deliver targeted and context-relevant promotional content.

Emotion Recognition

Implement emotion recognition to understand customer sentiment and provide real-time behavioral insights for targeted marketing.

Engagement Measurement

Measure engagement levels using behavioral cues such as attention and dwell time to quantify the impact of visual content.

Personalized Recommendations

Design a personalized advertisement recommendation engine that intelligently selects content based on multi-factor audience profiles.

Returning Customer Detection

Enable identification of returning customers using facial embedding techniques to support personalized loyalty experiences.

Actionable Insights

Provide meaningful data visualizations to improve retail decision-making and overall customer experience optimization.

Security Alert System

Detect unusual or suspicious behaviors and trigger real-time alerts for enhanced safety within the mall environment.
